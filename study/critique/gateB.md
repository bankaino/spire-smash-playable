**Why it still reads as a prototype (blunt):**
- DIFFICULTY CLIFF (biggest): ad mode = `SIM.spawn(0)` player, not the demo bot (demo: fire 12.3 s, 0 deaths, irrelevant to users). Casual model: 86% die once (median 3.2 s after press, ring 10), 83% end on "SO CLOSE!" at median 7.1 s / ring 18 / 28% of tower; only 17% ever see fire or the black wall. Blind always-hold dies at ring 8 / 1.68 s; tap patterns lose in 4-10 s. Cause: from ring 4 a 120 deg wedge, hopping 150 deg every 2 rings, at 8 rings/s -> blind survival (2/3)^(rings/2). The rule itself is fair/readable (pink rim, gloss, slow-mo "LET GO!" gives 0.67 s) but warn fires once per game, and `threat` pulses ALL rims (shared rimMat) only if already holding.
- VARIANT PLUMBING BUG: T_CLEAR/A_W1/A_W2/N are consumed in `build()` at load; build_playable.py does `Object.assign(SIM.K,..)` afterwards -> such overrides are silently ignored (verified: RINGS unchanged). fast_wow (START_RING 14) deletes the 4 clear tutorial rings and fire stays 18% (median 9.6 s). big_fire wastes a long early fire, then strands the player at the wall (win 6%, 45% time-out on the easy tower; wall 48-59 needs the 2nd fire from clear zone 38-47).
- HOOK: "HOLD TO SMASH" pill (y .243-.298 H) covers the ball top in ~75% of its hop (ball y .29-.34 H, r 76); same for "RELEASE ON BLACK" (.30 H). Idle viewer = 12 s of a hopping ball, then "KEEP SMASHING!" + "0 RINGS SMASHED"; the world never shows the verb before the first touch (hand presses, nothing happens).
- CALLOUT COLLISIONS: "ON FIRE!"+"x10!" every run (FIRE_N 10 == streak 10, same frame, y .30/.34), "LEVEL COMPLETE!"+x30 ghost, "OUCH!" over fading "LET GO!". Fire trigger = a text pop (120 px = 43 px on a 390 phone), no hit-stop/flash; heat arc is 15 px (5 css px) and nobody is told what fills it.
