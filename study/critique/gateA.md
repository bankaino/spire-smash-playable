# Gate A critique (3D world only; HUD stub ignored). Measured on frames/probe (1080x2337) + probe.mjs + SIM.at
**Scores /10:** hook 6 | clarity 7 | feel/juice 4 | visuals vs ref 6 | tech readiness 7 | overall 5.5
**Budget OK:** draws 11-22 (<=45), tris 74-118k (<=150k), hero 6.8-8.7% H (spec 6-8), tower 63% W (ref 55-62). Demo: smash 1.36 s, fire 12.26 s, finish 14.84, end ~16.4, 0 deaths. Dist is STALE (report still lists worker 207 KB + coin; rebuild).
**Why it still reads as a prototype (blunt):**
- Smash = 12 huge flat slabs/ring (~240x130 px each, 96 live at fire peak) instead of confetti: 30% of rows 400-1500 is yellow debris, ball ~15-30% visible in fire.jpg. Ref = hundreds of 20-30 px cubes; ours 12 chips/ring at 10-23 px. Lava pool max 80 < 96 -> Pool.add silently drops the NEWEST ring (impact frame has no debris).
- Camera is dead: smash shake = 0.16*tr^2 = 0.014 m = 2.7 px (1 px on a 390-wide phone), no FOV/zoom punch; fire = only zoom 0.94. Ball sits at 46% (rest) -> 57.5% (fire): camera lags 229 px, lookahead lost in the wow beat.
- Lighting is vector-flat: top face L/R pixels identical (253,206,64), ring-face stdL 23.7 (ref 50-62). No env map, no tone mapping anywhere -> "glossy obsidian" is mean L 15 / std 10, zero reflection (metalness 0.35 with nothing to reflect). No rim light, no AO at the pole.
- Hero/world contrast is weak: yellow ring vs orange sky dRGB 68 (ref blue/teal 195-220); lower orange rings vs pink sky ~100. Pole is beige (230,212,202 -> 199 right side) vs ref pure 250 white and fills the top 38% of the frame.
- Background is clutter, not richness: clouds = 11% of frame (17.8% of side bands), L>205 ~ pole L 225, so they compete with the hero; 1 cloud mesh x8, 1 island mesh x7, all same size/brightness, no haze. 5/15 props (clouds z-12/-15/-18, islands z-16/-21) recycle every +-11..13 m but the view half-height at their depth is 11.2-14.9 m -> visible pop.
- Rings read as stacked coins: TH 0.22 vs GAP 0.60 leaves 0.38 m scalloped sky gaps in the silhouette; bevel 0.028 m = 5 px (2 px on phone) is invisible. Trail = 6 translucent spheres (bubble chain); speed lines 4.7 px at alpha .22 look like scratches; death = 16 cubes, no flash, ball just vanishes.
- Finish/endcard: 80 m pole continues BELOW the platform (finish.jpg); a stray blob shadow floats mid-air under the ball on both endcards (blob not hidden at ST.end>=0); polar checker collapses to a grey moire hub; podium is a plain drum; dim 0.62 turns the world mud-brown/grey (pole L 225->101); confetti = flat unlit MeshBasic.
